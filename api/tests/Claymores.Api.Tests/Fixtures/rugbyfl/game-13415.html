Jacksonville Rugby Football Club
Established 1972 - Jacksonville - Duval
The Jacksonville Rugby Football Club is a Division II team and member of the Florida Rugby Union. We compete in the South region of USA Rugby along with teams from Alabama, Arkansas, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina and Tennessee. Over the years, the men’s club has been a leader in Florida rugby. Recently, Jacksonville RFC has been Florida Cup finalists, Florida matrix winners, USA Rugby South Champions, and National Final 8 participants. Current members have founded and coached the University of North Florida, Jacksonville Axemen Rugby League, Jacksonville women, and Jacksonville Bullydogs under-18 rugby teams. Jacksonville RFC prides itself as being leaders in the Jacksonville community as well. The club has over 100 active and past members representing a wide range of professions including lawyers, doctors, dentist, police officers, businessmen, engineers, scientists, military personnel, tradesman and others. We also have affiliations with Habijax and Adopt a Highway programs.
